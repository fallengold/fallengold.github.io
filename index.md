## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. <a href="https://www.danxurgb.net" style="color: #0076df;">Dan, XU</a>. I received my undergraduate degree from HKUST, where I studied Mathematics and Computer Science. My research interests include multimodal large language models, 3D graphics, embodied AI, and robotics.

Alongside my research interests, I have hands-on experience in multimodal AI and robotics. I worked on multimodal large language models as an intern at SenseTime, and during my undergraduate studies, I served as the embedded systems lead (PIC) for HKUST ENTERPRIZE, the university's RoboMaster team.
